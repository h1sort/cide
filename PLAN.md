## 1. The idea in one paragraph

A callback to Carver's *What We Talk About When We Talk About Love*: four people at a kitchen table can't agree on what love is. Today everyone says "agentic" and nobody means the same thing. The room is lawyers, regulators and bankers, so the most useful thing is shared vocabulary. We walk six concepts; each gets **what it is · what it looks like in a bank · a question to fight about**. Every question bends toward the thesis Andrés Trejo set up in the promo: the model can be copied, the data can't, so the real argument is who gets access to the data, on what terms and at what regulatory cost.

**Closing line:** *Cuando hablamos de banca agéntica, hablamos de tres cosas: quién puede leer, quién puede actuar y quién responde.*

## 3. Sources (§6 in datapub terms: every number on a slide needs one)

- **$148,000 fraud case**: Carlos's own example from the Claude for Finance meetup (illustrative, not a real customer).
- **Ley Fintech, Art. 76** (DOF, 9 Mar 2018): APIs for three data layers: datos financieros abiertos, agregados, transaccionales (with customer consent). Secondary rules were due within 24 months. https://sdv.com.mx/compendio/ley-para-regular-las-instituciones-de-tecnologia-financiera/articulo-76/ · https://www.revistaabogacia.com/el-open-finance-y-la-recuperacion-del-poder-de-los-usuarios-sobre-su-informacion-financiera/
- **Only open-data rules published (2020)**; aggregated and transactional still pending in 2026; amparo against CNBV, Banxico and SHCP in progress. https://facephi.com/observatory/open-banking-biometria-facial-cnbv-mexico/ · https://facephi.com/observatory/en/fintech-law-in-mexico-2026-competition-sandbox-and-new-financial-models/ · Banxico Circular 2/2020.
- **Visa / Plaid**: announced Jan 2020 at US$5.3B; DOJ sued to block on Nov 5, 2020 (Plaid as a nascent competitor); abandoned Jan 12, 2021. https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/visa-and-plaid-abandon-merger-after-antitrust-division-s-suit-block
- **"El modelo se puede replicar. Los datos no."** and the four debate questions: Andrés Trejo's LinkedIn post promoting the taller (Nov 2026).
- **Ask / act / judge**: Carlos's framework from "Fantastic Agents and Where to Find Them" (h1sort.com/slides/fantastic-agents/).
